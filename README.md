Sherpa-logistics

A turn-based survival game set in the alps coded in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Rations, oxygen and pitons to reach the summit over three summits, avoiding moving blizzards.

## Summits in the region
- PEAK 01: FROSTBITE'S EDGE

- PEAK 02: SERAC WALL

- PEAK 03: DEATH ZONE

![Sherpa-logistics gameplay screenshot](gameplay.png)

## Game
- It costs 1 oxygen per move.
- Snow fields 1 ration.
- Steep ridges, 2 rations
- Ice walls require 1 ration and 1 piton.
- Supply caches replenish rations, oxygen and pitons to your max levels.
- Blizzard rows move every turn, and drain 1 extra oxygen if caught outside.
- Setting up a tent costs 1 ration, gives you up to 2 oxygen and protects you from blizzard damage that turn. You can't set up camp with 1 or fewer rations.
- Win by making it to the summit, or lose if your oxygen or rations run out.
- Store best turn counts per peak in localStorage in browser

## Controls
- Move: WASD, Arrow keys, or click an adjacent tile
- Pitch tent: T, Space, or click PITCH TENT
- Restart peak: R or click RESTART
- Hover over tiles to see move costs and storm warnings
- Click AUDIO to turn sound effects on or off

## Run Locally
Open index.html in a web browser.
